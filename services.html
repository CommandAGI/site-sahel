Services

Five kinds of human work, sold as finished results.

A customer gets graded output, not a pile of hours. Each service below says what you receive, how the work runs through agents on CommandAGI, and where a person checks it.

Data labeling

You get: labeled images, text, audio clips or documents in the format your training run reads, with a label set and a guide you approved.

How it runs: an agent on CommandAGI splits the batch, sends each item to graded seats, and collects two labels on a sample. A reviewer resolves any pair that disagrees.

Cost: priced on request.

RLHF ranking

You get: pairwise and list rankings of model replies, with a short reason for each choice and a consistency score per worker.

How it runs: comparisons are served in blind pairs. Gold items with known answers are mixed in, and a worker whose gold accuracy drops is pulled from the queue until a lead reviews them.

Cost: priced on request.

Screen tasks

You get: step records of a software flow: what was on the screen, what was clicked, what the result was, and where the screen did not match the script.

How it runs: workers follow a written script in a sandbox you name. Each run is recorded and a second pass checks the record against the screen capture.

Cost: priced on request.

Verification

You get: a yes, no or cannot-tell for each claim, answer or extracted field, with the source passage that decided it.

How it runs: two checkers work each item independently. Agreement is recorded; disagreement goes to a verification lead who writes the ruling.

Cost: priced on request.

Transcription

You get: time-stamped transcripts with speaker turns, unclear words marked in brackets, and a glossary of terms you supplied.

How it runs: an agent makes a first draft from the audio. A worker corrects it against the audio, and a reviewer samples each file.

Cost: priced on request.
