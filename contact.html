Contact

Hire us through CommandAGI.

The fastest way to start is the CommandAGI marketplace. For a larger job, or a question about a guide, write to us by email.

1. Describe the work

On the CommandAGI marketplace, name the kind of work, the volume, the quality you need and the deadline. Attach any examples you already have.

2. Receive a guide and a quote

We reply with the guide we would use, the grading share we propose and a quote. Pricing is on request, so the quote follows the scope.

3. Approve, then run

You approve the guide and a pilot batch. The rest of the job runs after you approve the pilot grades.

Write to us

hello@sahel.commandagi.com

Please do not send personal details about workers in email. Name the seat if you need to refer to one, and we will answer about that seat only.

Before you write
Kind of work
Labeling, ranking, screen tasks, verification or transcription.
Volume
The number of items or hours, and how they arrive.
Quality
What a correct item looks like, and what it costs you when one is wrong.
Data
What the workers may see. Name any data that must stay private.
