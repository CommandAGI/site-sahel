The pool

Seventeen seats, each one a person and a grade.

Our workers are held as anonymized seats. A seat has a grade for each kind of work and a record of the tasks it did. The company does not store a worker's personal details on the seat.

One dot per worker seat in the current pool. A seat's colour carries no meaning.

What is kept on a seat

Seat grades

A grade for each kind of work: labeling, ranking, screen, verification, transcription. Each grade moves with reviewer evidence and carries its reason.

Work record

The tasks a seat did, the guide version each applied, and the reviewer grade on each. A worker can ask to see their own record.

Verification state

Whether the seat's identity verification is complete. Paid work waits for this. The state is shown as one of a small set of plain words.

Identity and pay

Identity first, then paid work.

Identity verification is still being built out, and we will describe each step here as it is set. Until a seat's verification is complete, no paid task is assigned to it, and no payment is settled to it. A seat may be held in the pool with a state that says why.

Pay follows the grade on each task. Rates for each kind of work are set in the guide for that work, and we publish them to the seat before its first paid task.

Labor standards

A grade is a reason, not a verdict on a person.

Our labor standards advisor reviews pay rules and the grading process. Workers can ask for a review of any grade, and a second reviewer who did not grade the item decides it.
