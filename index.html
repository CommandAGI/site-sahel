What we do

Four kinds of work, one way of checking it.

Labeling and ranking

Images, text and audio get the labels and the order that a model needs. Reviewers check a sample of every batch against the written guide.

Screens and transcription

Workers read a screen, a page or a recording and record exactly what is there, with time stamps and doubt marked, not guessed.

Verification

Second-pass checks: does this answer match its source, does this label match its image, does this claim hold. Disagreements go to a lead.

A task is done when a second person can repeat the check without asking the first one.

How a task moves

  1. Scoped

    A customer on the CommandAGI marketplace describes the work and the quality it needs. We write the guide and the examples first.

  2. Routed

    The task goes to seats whose grade and skills match. A worker with no grade for that work does not see it.

  3. Graded

    Reviewers grade a share of the output. Each worker's grade moves with that evidence, and every grade has a reason written beside it.

  4. Settled

    Payment follows the grade and waits until the worker's identity verification is complete. Nothing is paid before that.
